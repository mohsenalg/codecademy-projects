# Life Expectancy By Country

Comparing life expectancy between low-GDP and high-GDP countries using quartiles.

**What I did**
- Found the quartiles of life expectancy for all countries.
- Split countries at the median GDP into low and high GDP groups.
- Compared the quartiles of each group and plotted both groups on one overlapping histogram.

**Skills:** pandas, NumPy quantiles, histograms.

Dataset: `country_data.csv` from Codecademy (not included here).
