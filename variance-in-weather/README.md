# Variance in Weather

Mean, variance and standard deviation of London temperatures in 2015.

**What I did**
- Calculated the mean, variance and standard deviation of hourly temperature.
- Compared June and July, then printed the mean and standard deviation for every month.
- Plotted a bar chart of the average temperature for each 3-month period.

**Skills:** pandas, NumPy, spread (variance, standard deviation), matplotlib.

Data: `weather.p` (pickled pandas DataFrame of 2015 London weather from Codecademy), loaded by `weather_data.py`. It was pickled with an old pandas version, so a recent pandas may not be able to load it.
